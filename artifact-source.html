    Questions
    
Before you join


    

      

        Q1Do I have to be a Hanyang student?
        
Exchange students, degree students, language-institute students and KGSP scholars are all welcome. If you're studying at Hanyang in any form this semester, you can order. 교환학생·학위과정·어학당 모두 가능합니다.

      

      

        Q2What if my country isn't in the list?
        
Pick "Other" and email us at wpalskdl03@gmail.com to tell us which flag you want. We can source almost any national flag patch — the same 8-person rule applies to it.

      

      

        Q3What if fewer than 8 people pick my flag?
        
A flag patch is only produced when 8 or more people in the order choose the same flag. If your flag doesn't reach 8, your jacket is made with your sleeve text only and no flag — nothing else changes. 같은 국기 8명 미만이면 국기 없이 소매 문구만 들어갑니다.

      

      

        Q4Which characters can I use for the sleeve text?
        
English letters, Korean, Chinese characters, and these symbols: ^!@#$%&*()_=~/. Up to 9 characters, spaces allowed. Digits and other punctuation can't be embroidered. 영어·한글·한자·특수문자 ^!@#$%&*()_=~/ 만 가능, 최대 9자.

      

      

        Q5Can I skip the sleeve text and the flag?
        
Yes. Leave both empty and you get the plain jacket with only the back arch. The price is exactly the same — customization is included, not an add-on.

      

      

        Q6Can I change my size, or cancel?
        
Email the organizer at wpalskdl03@gmail.com and we'll change it. Once the jackets go into production, sizes are locked and orders can't be cancelled.

      

      

        Q7What if I leave Korea before week 4?
        
International shipping isn't realistic for a group order like this — the shipping and customs cost more than the jacket. Ask a friend who stays in Korea to receive it for you, and give us their address in advance.
